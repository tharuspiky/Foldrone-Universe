FOLDRONE / PRIVACY

Privacy is part of
the experience.

Foldrone aims to collect and use only information that is necessary for the operation of a particular service. The exact information handled can differ between products and connected third-party services.

Website visits

The public Foldrone website is designed to work without requiring an account. Hosting and security infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver, protect and maintain the website. Any analytics or external service used on a particular property should be described by that property's applicable notice.

Product data

Different Foldrone products may have different data flows. Before a product collects account, document, media, payment or other user data, its own privacy information should explain what is collected, why it is used, where it is processed and how long it is retained.

Updates

This privacy page will be updated when Foldrone's data practices materially change. The effective date shown in a future revision will identify the version that applies.

Important: This public overview is not a substitute for product-specific privacy notices where those are required.
